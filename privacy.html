Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Trello Time in List ("the Power-Up") is a Trello Power-Up developed by frethax. This privacy policy explains how the Power-Up handles your data.

Data We Access

The Power-Up accesses Trello card action history (list movement events and card creation events) solely to calculate how long a card has been in its current list and its total lifetime. This data is fetched in real time from the Trello API and is never stored on our servers.

Data Storage

The Power-Up does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All processing happens client-side in your browser. No account registration is required.

Third-Party Services

The Power-Up uses the Trello REST API to retrieve card action data. Your use of Trello is governed by Atlassian's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

The Power-Up does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.
